**Recommended investigation:**

  `parseFixedRoute`). With `LOG_LEVEL=debug`, a passing `TC604` would log

- Hypothesis: when no cached models exist, the proxy forward path runs the auth check

  `handleModelsRequest`); one of those branches may have a flaky null/empty check that
